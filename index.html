The problem

The cruising crisis

Up to 30% of peak-hour neighbourhood traffic comes from drivers circling blocks to find a slot, while countless driveways and off-peak bays sit empty and invisible.

“Parking search is the largest source of urban congestion.”Wasted fuel, higher costs and local emission spikes in dense neighbourhoods.
🚗

Endless circling

Drivers burn minutes and fuel looking for a space they cannot see.

🏠

Idle private space

Residential driveways and commercial bays stay vacant for most of the day.

🌫️

Dirty air

Idling engines cause localised emission spikes that harm air quality.

Technology

Lightweight by design

A progressive web app: zero app-store friction for hosts and drivers.

📱 PWA (HTML5 · CSS · JS)🔥 Firebase real-time store🗺️ OpenStreetMap + Leaflet🔄 Slot state machine

Available

The slot is open and visible to nearby drivers.

Reserved

Locked by one driver, with a unique pass issued.

Occupied

The vehicle has arrived; the slot is released when it leaves.

What’s next

GPS geofencing to auto-release slots when a vehicle departs, and links with municipal ward open-data feeds for public parking spaces.
